### What was done

- One template per outcome kind and notice kind, in one checked-in list; `satisfies Record<…>` plus `never`
  switches make a new kind fail typecheck. Console-only outcomes, duplicates and `nothing` map to named
  "no reply" templates.
- Readback: every line with quantity, unit, unit price and line amount, pickup or delivery (fee apart), then the
  total or "El total te lo confirmo", then "¿Está bien así? Respondé Confirmar, Corregir o Cancelar." with three
  buttons. Restricted lines say "solo lo vendemos aquí en la tienda" and are never priced.
- "¿A qué hora está?": armando "Todavía estamos armando tu pedido."; por_confirmar adds the typed choices;
  confirmado/preparando "Vas tercero en la lista. Te aviso apenas esté listo."; listo, entregado, cancelado; no
  open order. Never a time.
- Claims: `ClaimCheck { anyOf: Claim[] }`. First check against the outcome before the save; second check against
  `verifyClaims` right before the send. Failure → safe reply "Dejame revisar bien y ya te confirmo.", a
  `pendiente_raised` log line, `claims_blocked_at_ms` on the row, later parts of that reply failed.
- Typed words: confirmar/corregir/cancelar alone, any case, accents or punctuation. Button IDs are operation keys and
  read back into `CustomerAction`s; IDs that aren't ours give null.
- Buttons through `MessagingChannel.sendButtons`; payload shape checked against Meta's reply-button page on Oct 30
  (fetched; quoted in `client.ts`). Too many buttons, a title over 20 or repeated, an ID over 256 or a body over 1024
  → plain text with the typed fallback.

### Not done, risks, open questions

- **Not wired to a handler yet.** Nothing calls `replyForOutcome` in production: the placeholder handler still
  answers. Notices aren't routed to customer conversations (needs the customers work). Typed words and taps
  aren't turned into engine calls in a handler; resolving typed "confirmar" to "the readback version last shown"
  (gap g) is not built (the stored claims on sent rows could serve).
- **Pendientes only logs.** Nobody sees a blocked reply until gap (c) lands.
- **Safe reply after a cancel.** A reply blocked because the customer cancelled still sends "Dejame revisar bien y
  ya te confirmo." (the issue asks for the safe reply on every block); the cancel's own reply follows it.
- **Pretend engine:** `verifyClaims` answers only order state and total; price, stock, payment and balance claims
  always block against it. Customer `correct_order` and substitute answers return `nothing`.
- **Merge:** `test/secrets.test.ts` lists migrations, so it will conflict with branches adding 0004/0005.
- **If the engine's total includes the delivery fee**, every readback with a fee fails the first check (the
  proposal says it doesn't).

Contract questions:

1. Gap (c): should Pendientes be raised through a `Commit` for the caller's batch (proposal) or a port like
   `Pendientes.raise`? At dispatch there is no job batch to join.
2. Price claims: `unit_price` has no order or version, so it checks today's catalog. Add a `line_price` claim
   `{ orderId, version, lineId, price }`, or confirm that the version claim pins line prices.
3. A claim for "one of these states" (used here as `anyOf` on our side), or should `verifyClaims` take it?
4. Gap (f): no outcome for store hours; no template.
5. Gap (g): keys for corregir, cancelar and the substitute buttons (`correct:`, `cancel:`, `substitute:…:<line>`,
   `remove:…:<line>`) are this branch's proposal; only `confirm:` is in the contract.
6. `order_changed` has no previous quantities, so "azúcar 1 lb, 3 (antes 2)" can't be said; the full readback
   goes instead.
7. `ask` with `same_place` has no place ID or text, so the Sí / Otro lugar buttons can't be built.
8. `AnswerFact` has no product name: answers echo the customer's words.
9. What outcome does `correct_order` produce?
10. Gap (j) as proposed: confirm it, including "fee not in the total".
11. The real engine must authorize (actor, and the order belongs to this customer) before answering a repeated key
    from `operations`: button keys are predictable, so a replayed key must never return another customer's outcome.
12. Queue position: add a `queue_position` claim, or drop the ordinal ("Vas tercero") and say only "está en la lista".
13. "No tenés ningún pedido abierto" claims an absence, which no `Claim` can express (every claim names an order,
    product, payment or balance); it goes out unchecked. Add a `no_open_order { customerId }` claim?
14. `payment_rejected` names no order and there is no claim for a rejected payment; it goes out unchecked.
