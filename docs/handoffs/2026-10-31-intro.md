## Decisions for Angel

1. **"🙋 Hablar con alguien" is 21 characters** by our button check (the emoji counts two UTF-16 units), so
   WhatsApp buttons would be dropped. The fallback is **"✋ Hablar con alguien"** (20). It's also used when the
   owner's first name is too long ("🙋 Hablar con " leaves room for 6 letters). Meta's own counting rule is unknown.
2. **Control word as first message:** one bubble, greeting + answer. ALTO and deletion end with
   `_Tus datos: {url}_` only (an ALTO line after they said ALTO reads oddly); a handoff ends with the full footer.
   The ALTO answer still carries the stop's existing "escribí: quiero hablar con…" line (stop copy unchanged, as
   asked).
3. **A job with no reply:** an earlier message of a burst no longer sends the intro on its own (the burst's reply
   carries it); only the burst's newest job with no reply sends the welcome alone.
4. **Claim blocked at dispatch:** if the merged reply's claim fails the second check, the safe reply goes instead
   and the greeting goes with it (the customer is still marked introduced). Rare; not handled.
5. The privacy page (`src/interfaces/http/privacy.ts`) still says "asistente automático… no una persona" and
   teaches "quiero hablar con…". Left as is: it's the notice, not the intro.
